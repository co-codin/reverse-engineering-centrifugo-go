channeld is a single process. History lives in that process and is not shared across pods.

Forward the service:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "channeld.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.port }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}

WebSocket: ws://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.service.port }}/connection/websocket
Health:    http://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.service.port }}/health
Publish:   POST http://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.service.port }}/api/publish
